## Patterns to watch

- A question that two or more testers find confusing: rewrite it.
- A session where several groups take far longer than 75 minutes: trim questions, or move a Looking to Christ passage to a callout.
- Check button comments: note whether answers get "Well done" too easily (see the parked item on tightening keyTerms) or whether sensible answers are marked as off-target.
- Testers who dislike a theological point: record the passage and the wording, and I will check the plan's Part 3 for what was decided and why before you decide.
